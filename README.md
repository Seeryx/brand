# Seeryx brand assets

Logos and the social preview of Seeryx, a prediction market on Solana (https://seeryx.com). These files are for press,
directories and partners; they may be used to refer to Seeryx as it is, not altered, recoloured or combined with other
marks.

| File | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | the mark (lime rounded square, black trend arrow), vector | any |
| `logo-512.png` | the mark | 512 × 512 |
| `logo-400.png` | the mark, for directories that ask for 400 × 400 | 400 × 400 |
| `logo-200.png` | the mark, small | 200 × 200 |
| `social-preview-1200x630.png` | the link preview picture of seeryx.com | 1200 × 630 |

Direct links (raw): `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seeryx/brand/main/logo-400.png` and the same for the other files.

Colours: lime `#d6ff3d` on dark backgrounds (`#8ab800` on light ones), black `#000000`. Wordmark: lowercase "seeryx",
"ryx" in lime.

One sentence: Seeryx is a non-custodial prediction market on Solana: crypto Up/Down markets from 5 minutes to a day and
event markets resolved by UMA, traded in USDC with no SOL needed.
